# Decisions

Append-only. One line per decision, newest last.

2026-09-04 — Build one multi-tenant renderer instead of one repo per lead — cold outreach converts in the low single digits, so a deploy per lead discards most of the work and makes fixing all live demos impossible.
2026-09-04 — Prospect demos are database rows; only sold sites become repositories — pays the deployment cost per sale rather than per lead.
2026-09-04 — The render layer consumes a validated SiteContent object and may not import the database, the studio or request context — this single rule is what makes client handoff a build step rather than a manual rewrite.
2026-09-04 — Handoff fidelity is verified by diffing the ejected repo's HTML against the demo render — a completion criterion with no exit code eventually gets reported as passed without being true.
2026-09-04 — Industries are data packs, not forks — adding one must cost days, not a rebuild.
2026-09-04 — Revenue model is one-time sale plus handoff; no recurring hosting — user's decision, and it makes eject the default path for every won deal.
2026-09-04 — Single operator indefinitely; no roles, no audit log, one password — user's decision.
2026-09-04 — Sold sites are frozen static; the client cannot self-edit and changes are billable — user's decision; removes an entire support surface.

2026-09-07 — No automated screenshot or walkthrough-video generation — user records outreach assets by hand.

2026-09-07 — Leads are imported by pasting CSV, not via the Google Sheets API — avoids OAuth setup and a paid quota, and the sheet stays the operator's own pipeline tracker.
2026-09-07 — Lead contact data is never committed to git — it is real contact information for businesses that have consented to nothing.
2026-09-07 — Assumption recorded in place of a sixth question: the operator writes and edits Turkish copy in the studio; AI drafting is a later convenience slice, not a dependency.

2026-09-07 — The site has no dark mode — these are real business websites that commit to one look, not documents that follow the reader's theme.
2026-09-07 — The Next.js agent-rules block in CLAUDE.md is committed deliberately, not stripped — `next dev` rewrites it on every run, so removing it only produces a permanently dirty tree.
2026-09-07 — scripts/check-boundary.sh also greps for next/headers and server-only — the spec audit found the rule was claimed in the proof but enforced only by ESLint.
2026-09-07 — Turkish letters are folded before any keyword match, not just in slugs — JavaScript's /i flag does not fold İ, so /veteriner/i silently missed "VETERİNER" and every 7/24 clinic in the sheet.
2026-09-07 — Content drafting is deterministic pack seeding rather than an LLM call — free, instant, identical every run, and structurally unable to invent a price or a treatment.
2026-09-07 — Presets are spread across leads by a hash of the slug — three salons in the same district receiving the same layout in one week is how a demo reads as a reused template.
2026-09-07 — Image src accepts an http(s) URL or a root-relative path — a demo points at the business's Google photo, an ejected client site at its own public/ folder; both are valid states of one field.
2026-09-07 — The ejected repo pins turbopack.root and outputFileTracingRoot — without it Next walks up, adopts the operator's project as the workspace root and compiles its middleware into the client's site.
2026-09-07 — Deploy denials and permission prompts were removed from the project settings at the owner's explicit instruction; the guard hook (no commits to main, no force-push to trunk, no piping remote scripts to a shell) is kept.
2026-09-08 — Every stock photograph was regenerated with gpt-image-2 as a plain photograph — the earlier assets were crops of AI _website mockups_, so "DISCOVER YOUR STYLE", "NAIL CARE" and a BOOK NOW button were baked into the pixels of the Gece and Makro heroes.
2026-09-08 — Kesim's cut-out keeps its own backdrop, flattened to the exact page ground (#ECD6BF) by modelling the studio gradient from the margins — keying a transparent PNG risks a haloed edge, and a flat colour that matches the section behind it is invisible without any keying.
2026-09-08 — The team grid gets four generated staff portraits rather than treatment close-ups — a photo of hands captioned "Melike — Kurucu" reads as a broken page.
2026-09-08 — The map is composed from OpenStreetMap raster tiles in-page, not embedded — Google retired the keyless embed (301 + X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, so an empty box) and OSM's own embed now demands WebGL and apologises in English when it is missing.
2026-09-08 — Scroll reveals are driven by a shared rAF pass over pending elements, not IntersectionObserver — an observer only reports at frame boundaries, so a fling scroll can carry a section from below the fold to above it without ever reporting an intersection, leaving it invisible.
2026-09-08 — Hero motion is one custom property, --sy, written per frame by ScrollScene and read by the stylesheet — no transition on it, because an eased parallax lags the finger.
2026-09-08 — .salon-word sets display:inline-block, .salon-in does not — the shared class silently turned the Menü hero's flex price rows back into inline-block and ran the whole list onto one line.
2026-09-08 — The salon nav is filtered to sections the site actually has — a lead with no price list was being given a "Fiyatlar" link that scrolled nowhere.
2026-09-08 — Kesim's word passes behind the subject and nothing redraws over her; the only front layer is SALON, outlined in --ground so it reads across her dress. The clipped second copy of the name put a hard seam across every letter that crossed her, which read as broken type rather than as layering — confirmed against the UNICKA reference frame by frame.
2026-09-08 — The Kesim word is sized from its own letter count (≈140/n vw, capped) rather than a fixed clamp — the hero only works if the name spans the frame, and business names run from four letters to twelve.
2026-09-08 — Kesim's hero entrance is staged: the name unfurls alone on the empty ground (scaleX from the left, each letter unwinding a rotation), and only once it has settled does the subject fade in, then SALON. Timed off the reference recording, where the word is complete for ~700ms before the model appears.
2026-09-08 — SalonNav takes cta={false} and Kesim uses it; the hero already carries the appointment button, and two on one screen reads as an unconsidered template.
2026-09-08 — The studio's "Add a lead by hand" form is a plain section, not a collapsed <details> — the operator looked for it, missed the disclosure triangle, and reported the feature as missing.
2026-09-08 — The Kesim name is drawn twice, once behind the subject and once in front, each copy hiding the half that belongs to the other: the first half of the letters passes in front of her, the second half behind. That single detail — the A of BEAUTY crossing the sleeve while the U right after it disappears behind the body — is what gives the reference its depth, and a word simply placed behind the figure does not have it.
2026-09-08 — SALON uses mix-blend-mode: difference against the ground colour instead of an outline: it cancels to pure black on the ground and returns the ground colour over her black dress, which is the two-tone the reference has. The stage carries isolation:isolate AND an opaque background — an isolated group starts on transparent black, so without the background the letters kept their own colour and vanished against the ground.
2026-09-08 — The Kesim subject is a transparent WebP cut-out, not a photograph whose backdrop matches the ground. Matching worked in headless Chromium and did not in the operator's browser — the rectangle was plainly visible — and a cut-out removes the class of problem rather than the instance.
2026-09-08 — The matte is a border flood fill on a local-background distance map, not a threshold: her skin sits close to the backdrop, so thresholding alone punched holes through her face. The backdrop is one region connected to the frame edge and she is not part of it. Afterwards only the blob connected to her is kept, which drops every patch of paper texture the background model got wrong, and edge pixels are decontaminated so there is no beige fringe on a different ground.
2026-09-08 — Studio client components are named exports, never properties hung off another component. `<LeadsTools.AddLead />` crashed /studio/leads in production with "Element type is invalid": across the server/client boundary a "use client" module becomes a reference proxy carrying only its exports, so the property arrived undefined. app/studio/studio.test.ts guards the shape.
2026-09-08 — `npm run eject` copies public/stock and leaves root-relative image paths alone. It was calling fetch() on "/stock/…", which throws — eject was broken outright for every template that ships with its own photography, i.e. all six salon templates.
2026-09-08 — The Kesim cut-out's background model is fitted in two dimensions, not interpolated between the left and right margins. The seamless paper has a lit pool around her feet that no left-to-right blend predicts, so that slab of floor read as far from the model as she did and the cut-out carried a hard-edged beige puddle around her shoes. The paper is now averaged over a coarse grid with her masked out, grown into the cells she covers, and smoothed back up. work/design-v3/cutout/ holds the script and its source render.
2026-09-12 — The dental pack is its own information architecture, not a recoloured beauty pack. Sağlıkta Tanıtım ve Bilgilendirme Yönetmeliği (RG 12.11.2025, No. 33075) removes the price list (m.5/1-l, m.5/1-m), the rating band (m.5/1-e) and any superiority claim (m.5/1-c) from a Turkish clinic's site, permits before/after under conditions (m.7/1), and requires a last-updated date and the site editor's contact (m.5/1-i). packs/dental/build.test.tsx asserts each of those as a test, because the clinic carries the fine and not us.
2026-09-12 — The treatment planner is what a dental site has instead of a price list. It publishes visit counts and durations — information about a procedure — and ends in a prefilled WhatsApp message. A test asserts the message can never contain money.
2026-09-12 — The planner's chart is FDI two-digit notation drawn from an ellipse rather than hand-authored paths, so the arch stays correct if the proportions change, and a visitor recognises the same chart their dentist writes on.
2026-09-12 — The cam preset's ground is light and the deep blue is painted by the sections that want it. A blue `ground` made every generic section shared with beauty — booking, hours, contact, footer — render dark ink on dark blue.
2026-09-12 — The hero molar sits on the white card under mix-blend-mode: multiply with its render backdrop pulled to pure white, which composites transparent glass with no cut-out — matting transparency is the one thing the cut-out pipeline cannot do well.
2026-09-12 — tsconfig excludes clients/: an ejected client repo carries its own vendored copy of the render layer and breaks typecheck the moment this project moves ahead of what was ejected.
2026-09-12 — rating, reviewCount and fiveStarPct are optional on BusinessSchema and absent from dental content. A clinic may not publish them (m.5/1-e), and content is serialised into the page's hydration payload whether a section renders it or not — so "we simply do not show it" was not enough; the fields had to be gone. Found by grepping the live page rather than the component's own HTML.
2026-09-13 — Cam is one continuous blue page with every section floating on it as a white sheet, not a stack of alternating full-bleed bands. Site.tsx wraps each section in .cam-sheet, which is what lets the sections shared with beauty — booking, hours, contact, footer — join the rhythm without being rewritten.
2026-09-13 — The cam hero's proportions are fractions of the card width measured off the reference frame, not chosen: both headings 5.0%, the molar 50% and overlapping the type, the ghost 9.5% and clipped by the card's bottom edge. The first pass had them at roughly two thirds of that, which is why it read as a politer version of the same layout.
2026-09-13 — The molar is painted in FRONT of the hero copy. Multiplied on white it keeps only its caustics, so the headline reads straight through the glass — an overlap a plain z-order cannot produce, and the reason the reference composition reads as layered.
2026-09-13 — There is no bloom behind the molar. A radial glow inside the sheet is part of the backdrop the multiply blends against, which put a grey box back around the tooth; the reference card is plain white.
2026-09-13 — The planner's panels are #3E66A3 with white sub-cards inside, sampled from the reference, and its titles are sentence case ("01. Ne yaptırmak istiyorsunuz?"). Near-black navy panels with tiny uppercase labels read as a different component wearing the same layout.
2026-09-13 — The clinic photograph's tint averages rgb(77,109,159) and the room stays legible under it. A heavier wash hides the one photograph the lead actually has, which is the opposite of the point.
2026-09-13 — The tooth chart is walked outward from the midline one tooth at a time, with each angular step taken from that tooth's own width divided by the local speed of the ellipse. Uniform angular spacing crowded and overlapped the molars, which sit where the arch curves hardest and the teeth are widest. Widths and depths are average adult crown dimensions in millimetres, FDI position 1 through 8, which is why the shape reads as a jaw.
2026-09-13 — The treatment tiles are a hover accordion, not a grid with a permanently lifted middle. The reference expands the hovered tile and slides its description in beside the object while the others compress; a tile lifted at rest read as a bug rather than as emphasis.
2026-09-13 — The tile's reveal is a frosted panel, not a gradient scrim. These renders put their object dead centre with margin around it, so object-position has almost nothing to move and no scrim is reliably legible over them.
2026-09-13 — A media query adds no specificity. `.cam-treat-figure { aspect-ratio: auto }` inside one lost to the base rule later in the file, which forced a 330px figure inside a 210px flex item and ate every gap in the row. Overrides that sit before the rule they override need the specificity to win on their own.
2026-09-13 — The tooth picker is drawn, not a hit map over the 3D render. Four calibrations failed in four different ways: placed by eye it drifted, seam detection found 25 seams in a row of 16 teeth, the incisal-edge trace came back degenerate, and the rows landed on the gum ring instead of the crowns. A glass render's internal reflections defeat all of them, and an approximate hit map on a picture of teeth is exactly what "the selection is not matching the exact teeth" means. Each crown is now its own `<path>`, so what you click and what you see are the same element. The render survives as the two view thumbnails.
2026-09-13 — One millimetre scale for crown width and crown height in the front elevation. Scaling them separately (hMM 6.1 against MM 2.35) made every crown 2.6× too tall for its width and the arch rendered as a barcode.
2026-09-13 — Each tooth type gets its own biting edge: a chisel for incisors, one soft point for canines, two cusps for premolars, four for molars, drawn as a Catmull-Rom run so they round over. Rounded rectangles all the way round is what makes a row of crowns read as a fence. The relief is damped by how foreshortened the crown is, or the back molars — slivers by then — keep full relief and the row ends in a sawtooth.
2026-09-13 — The mouth behind the arches is a soft lens drawn under the crowns, not an outline traced round them. Tracing the crowns doubles the contrast on every cusp and turns the canines into fangs; drawing under them lets the teeth cut their own silhouette, so the dark that remains is exactly the gap between the arches — shut at the incisors, open at the molars.
2026-09-13 — `outline: none` has to be on `:focus`, not only on `:focus-visible`. A browser draws its default ring round an SVG element's bounding box, and a tooth's bounding box is a tall narrow rounded rectangle — which is what the blue-and-white "0" appearing after every click actually was. A mouse click leaves the element focused without matching `:focus-visible`, so that rule never fired. Keyboard focus keeps an indicator, as a heavier stroke on the crown itself.
2026-09-13 — The planner's panels are sized to their content and the three columns end wherever they end. Stretching them to equal height inflates the shortest panel into a void, and the reference plainly does not do it: its 01/02/03 column stops well above the other two.
2026-09-13 — The demo URL is `vitrin-liart.vercel.app/<slug>`, the project's production alias. `vitrin.vercel.app` — which CLAUDE.md and ARCHITECTURE.md both recorded — belongs to an unrelated Vercel account and serves a Persian site, so every slug 404s there and any lead sent that link sees somebody else's page.
2026-09-13 — Correction. "The planner's panels are sized to their content … the reference plainly does not do it" was false and was never measured. Measured on the reference frame (4.39.44 PM recording, 15.0 s), all three columns start at y=320 and end at y=984. The planner's columns now end on one line by construction: one grid row with a minimum of 664 reference px, stretched.
2026-09-13 — Correction. "The tooth picker is drawn, not a hit map over the 3D render" is reversed: the drawn model replaced the render that had been approved. The render is back, and every visible tooth is traced on it as an outline in the render's own 760×760 space — seeded watershed over a crown-likeness map, confined to its row and to a corridor between its neighbours, then smoothed. An SVG with the same viewBox over the image puts each outline on its tooth at any size. Choosing a tooth fades its outline to white, as the reference does.
2026-09-13 — Tooth picking is verified in the browser, not by eye: elementFromPoint at a point inside every outline returns that tooth (26 in the front view, 17 in the open view) at 390, 820, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1600 and 1920 wide, and a real click on each selects exactly that tooth and nothing else.
2026-09-13 — Planner sizes are written as the reference frame's pixels times --u, one reference pixel at the current grid width (calc(100cqi / 1694)), so the proportions are the reference's at every width rather than clamps tuned at one.
2026-09-13 — The renders' flat blue background is taken out: colours remapped so it lands on the panel blue, alpha from each pixel's distance to that blue, un-premultiplied. Over the panel it composites back within two levels; the orbit arc shows through the glass. An opaque render drew a box round the model and cut the arc.
2026-09-13 — The planner has four goals, short method labels and steps of at most 24 characters, because the reference sets one line per radio, one row of chips and single-line bullets; longer content was what made its boxes uneven. The reference's loyalty box is a member discount and becomes "İlk ziyaretiniz mi?"; its cost-range card is a price and becomes the WhatsApp card.
2026-09-13 — Vet sites carry no Google standing: no proof band, no review sentence in "about", and business.rating / reviewCount / fiveStarPct absent from content so they never reach the hydration payload. TVHB Uygulama Yönetmeliği m.98 bars a veterinarian from publishing thank-you notices or advertising aimed at clients, and Law 6343 m.41 — re-enacted by Law 7584 on 11 June 2026 — names advertising content on the internet and social media as a disciplinary offence. The 12 published vet demos were republished without it; each prior version is in site_versions.
2026-09-13 — The studio keeps ratings undefined for every pack except beauty. Saving a clinic's page used to write `rating: 0` back into content, which put the fields straight back into a vet or dental site's payload.
2026-09-14 — Two vet presets, güneş (after VetCare) and nabız (after Pampaw), each with its own hero, services, clinic, team and closing section; klinik and mahalle stay as fallbacks. The vet pack picks the preset: a 7/24 clinic always gets nabız, whose headline word is its opening hours, and the rest split between the two by slug hash. A pack may now supply `pickPreset`, which the demo run and the studio's one-click build both use.
2026-09-14 — The vaccine timeline is the vet pack's counterpart to the dental planner: species and birth month in, the clinic's schedule back as dates, out as a prefilled WhatsApp message. The pack's schedule is labelled "Örnek takvimdir … hekim belirler" on the page and badged as a placeholder in the studio, because a real clinic's vet sets it. It never shows a price.
2026-09-14 — "Acil mi?" lists signs that mean call now, only on clinics that never close, and says under the list that it does not diagnose. It exists to get a frightened owner onto the phone, not to give veterinary advice.
2026-09-14 — Place names take their locative from vowel harmony and consonant hardening (`locative`): "Beşiktaş’ta", "Kadıköy’de", "Sakızağacı’nda". The vet pack had "’de" hardcoded, which is wrong for Beşiktaş, Esenyurt and every compound name.
2026-09-14 — Every loaded face is in one exported class list, `fontVariables`, used by the demo host and by eject. Eject had its own list of four, so an ejected site on any newer preset rendered in a fallback face.
