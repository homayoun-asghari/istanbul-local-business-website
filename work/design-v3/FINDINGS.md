# What four beauty-salon themes actually do

372 frames extracted at 2fps from five recordings; all 20 contact sheets reviewed.

## The miss that started this

Not one design I produced contains a human being. Every one of these four themes leads with
a person. For a güzellik salonu the photograph of a face, hair, hands or nails **is** the
product shot — the storefront is not.

## KOSA — hair salon

Full-bleed dark photograph of a woman with flowing red hair. A giant ghosted wordmark
"KOSA" scrolls horizontally behind her as a marquee. Thin letterspaced uppercase headline
centred over the image, warm tan CTA. Demo variants named MODERN / ORGANIC / CLASSY, each a
different palette — sage and peach colour blocks, `H A I R` and `C A R E` set as widely
spaced capitals, scalloped image edges, vertical rotated section labels on the page edge.

## ATURA — nail salon

Extreme close-up crops: lips, a hand, nails. Nothing wide. Cross-fading hero slideshow.
**Script accent over serif capitals** — "nail fashion" handwritten above "ART MANICURE".
That pairing is the beauty signature.

Inner sections worth stealing:

- **Price menu**: round thumbnail · service name · dotted leader · price. Salons publish prices.
- A white card overlapping a full-bleed photo ("BOOK TIME TO GET MANICURE").
- Centred italic testimonial with one small round avatar.
- Counter stats, a partner logo strip on black, blog cards, a script watermark behind the
  newsletter.

## GLOWRIA — beauty and spa

The most sellable register for an Istanbul SMB. Blush ground, terracotta accent.

- **Two-tone headings**: black roman first line, terracotta italic serif second line.
- **Arch-topped image masks** — the rounded doorway shape.
- Collage hero: photo plus stat cards plus a cluster of round avatars reading "ask Happy
  Customers".
- Service cards with a numbered circular badge and "Learn More →".
- Floating white stat pills over a dark photo band.
- **Package cards with a price badge** and "Choose Plan".
- Team grid of four portraits with role and social dots.
- An Instagram strip of six squares above the footer.
- A booking form as a dark card beside a cream hours card.
- Botanical line illustrations as decoration.

## UNICKA — beauty and fashion salon

The most striking, and the most stealable technique.

- **Cut-out subject interleaved with giant type** — head in front of the letters, body behind.
- **Letterforms filled with an image** — "TATTOO" is clipped to a tattoo photograph.
- Flat greige ground, one hot pink, a small letterspaced word beneath the display word.
- Rotating hero: BARBER / TATTOO / BEAUTY, each with its own cut-out subject.
- Asymmetric photo mosaic with category labels overlaid.
- Team cards, a portrait strip carousel, and a newsletter over scattered ghosted words
  (CHEEKS, NOSE, LIPS, FRESH).

## The fifth recording

My own `krom` template. Next to these it is a dark empty room with a floating object and no
people in it. That is the whole problem in one frame.

## What every one of them has that none of mine do

1. A person, large, in the first screen.
2. A script or high-contrast serif somewhere — never a lone geometric sans.
3. Published prices.
4. A named team with portraits.
5. An Instagram strip.
6. A booking form on the page, not only a WhatsApp link.
7. Warm skin-tone palettes: blush, terracotta, greige, tan. Not one is cold.
